Help & Support

Need help with Tempo? Contact us about account access, app issues, sports information, privacy, or safety concerns.

Email support
jordanoliver@thetemposports.com

Email Tempo Support

If the button does not open your email app, copy the address above into your preferred email service.

Report an app issue

Include your Tempo username, device model, operating-system version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue. Screenshots can help; hide personal information and private conversations that are not needed to explain the problem.

For incorrect scores or sports information, include the sport, league, teams or athletes, game date, and the screen where you noticed it.

Account access

Use Forgot Password on the login screen to reset your password. If you still cannot sign in, email support with the account email or username. Never send your password, authentication tokens, or password-reset codes.

Report content or abusive behavior

Use the reporting option available on the relevant profile, post, comment, or message. You can also email us with the username, a description of the concern, and enough context to identify the content. Use the subject Tempo Safety Report for safety concerns.

You can block accounts through the app and manage blocked accounts in Settings → Blocked Accounts. For immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

Delete your account

In Tempo, open Settings → Delete Account and confirm using your current password. If you cannot access your account, email support for assistance. We may need to verify ownership before acting on an account request.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy questions, email us with the subject Tempo Privacy Request. Read our Privacy Policy for information about how the app handles data.
