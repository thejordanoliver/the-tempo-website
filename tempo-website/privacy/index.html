Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Tempo handles information when you use the Tempo sports app and contact our support team. For questions or privacy requests, email jordanoliver@thetemposports.com.

Information you provide

  • Account and profile information: your email address, username, password, and profile details you choose to add, such as your name, bio, profile photo, and banner. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than readable passwords.
  • Content and communications: posts, comments, chat messages, direct messages, poll responses, images, videos, GIF selections, and information you send to support or include in reports.
  • Preferences and activity: favorite teams and sports, follows, likes, predictions or votes, layout preferences, notification settings, and other interactions with app features.

Information collected through app use

Tempo processes account identifiers and session information to keep you signed in and connect your activity to your account. Search analytics include search text, search-session identifiers, results shown, selected results, result positions, and request timing. Our servers and infrastructure providers may also process connection information, such as IP addresses and request details, when delivering the service.

How we use information

  • Provide scores, sports content, personalized feeds, favorites, and social features.
  • Create and secure accounts, maintain sessions, and send password-reset emails.
  • Deliver messages, uploaded media, and in-app notifications.
  • Understand search performance and improve the app.
  • Respond to support requests, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and protect the service.

Who can see your information

Your profile and content shared in forums or game chats can be visible to other users. Direct messages and their attachments are intended for conversation participants; information involved in a report may also be reviewed to investigate abuse. Do not share sensitive information in public content. Other people may save or copy information you share with them.

Service providers and other disclosures

Tempo uses service providers for hosting, database storage, media processing and delivery, and email delivery. These providers process information needed to perform those functions. Uploaded media is processed through Cloudinary. GIF features use GIPHY content. Opening external news or other third-party links takes you to services with their own privacy practices.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, or secure the service.

Device permissions and local storage

When you choose to upload media, Tempo uses the media access you grant through your device. You can manage permissions in your device settings. Tempo stores session credentials in device secure storage on supported native platforms and uses local storage for preferences and cached app information. Signing out clears app-managed session information.

Retention and account deletion

Information is retained as needed to operate your account, deliver the features you use, and address security, support, and legal obligations. You can initiate account deletion in the app through Settings → Delete Account, using your current password. If you cannot access your account, contact us for assistance.

Contact us for details about retention or deletion of particular posts, messages, uploaded files, logs, or backups, and any information retained for legal or security reasons. Account deletion cannot remove copies that other people have independently saved.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your profile, manage favorites and app preferences, control available messaging and activity-status settings, block users, report content, and delete your account. You may also email us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to ask about rights available where you live. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Never send your password or reset codes.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has provided personal information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Security

Tempo uses safeguards such as password hashing and authenticated access to protect account information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as Tempo changes. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact
